Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about fixed-rate bonds, protection, and our free comparison service.

Fixed-rate bonds are secure investment vehicles where you deposit money with a financial institution for a set duration (e.g., 1 to 5 years) at a fixed, unalterable interest rate. Your capital is preserved, and you receive predictable interest either monthly, annually, or as a lump sum upon maturity.

Yes. All institutions featured on CompareBondRates.co.uk are authorised and regulated in the UK. Eligible deposits with UK banking groups are protected under the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS) up to statutory limits of £120,000 per person per authorised banking institution.

Minimum investments start from as low as £5,000, while premier institutional rates (7.45% - 8.20% p.a.) typically begin from £10,000 to £25,000. Our comparison tools help you identify the highest paying rates for your exact budget.

Our service is 100% free for investors. We receive an intermediary commission directly from issuing financial institutions when a customer opens an account. This never reduces your interest rate or affects your principal capital in any way.
