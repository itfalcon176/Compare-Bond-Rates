Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about fixed-rate bonds, protection, and our free comparison service.

Fixed-rate bonds are secure investment vehicles where you deposit money with a financial institution for a set duration (e.g., 1 to 5 years) at a guaranteed, unalterable interest rate. Your capital is preserved, and you receive predictable interest either monthly, annually, or as a lump sum upon maturity.
